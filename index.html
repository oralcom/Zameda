How do I request a consultation?

Are consultations private?

We treat consultation conversations with discretion and encourage clients to share only information they are comfortable providing.

Can I speak with you on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on this website to start a conversation directly with Zamani Spiritual Services.

Do you guarantee a particular result?

No. Spiritual services are personal in nature and outcomes cannot be guaranteed. We aim to provide respectful spiritual guidance and perspective.

Is spiritual guidance a replacement for professional care?

No. Spiritual guidance should not replace qualified medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice when those services are needed.
